The Advocate's Walk

Your story is somebody else's medicine

East — The Receiving

Dawn. First light. The new advocate enters.

You stand at the eastern gate and receive your first lamp: communication. Voice is the medicine of this direction.

Quests

  • Complete the Start Path onboarding
  • Read the handbook introduction
  • Send one real message to one real person in the Sitting Room
Threshold: The Receiving. You cannot cross until you've used your voice once — one genuine message, one genuine person.
Clan Mother's blessing: "May your words find the ears they were meant for."

Journal — "What called you to this path before you knew its name?"

South — The Kindling

Summer. Growing heat. The quests burn.

The handbook modules open one by one. Each quality is a flame you tend.

Quests

  • Complete handbook modules on communication, empathy, assertiveness, creativity, perseverance
  • Sit in the Sitting Room test
  • Name, in writing, which quality stretched you most
Threshold: The Kindling. No naming, no crossing. Reflection is the key.
Clan Mother's blessing: "What burns in you is what lights others."

Journal — "Which quality burned hottest in you, and what did it reveal?"

West — The Giving

Harvest. What you sow returns.

Your referral links pull weight. Your rank climbs. But the gate asks for generosity.

Quests

  • Activate your unique ref link
  • Record at least one conversion
  • Share one win — a story, a tip, a screenshot — back into the advocate circle
Threshold: The Giving. What you give is what you keep.
Clan Mother's blessing: "The harvest belongs to those who planted for others."

Journal — "What did you give away that came back multiplied?"

North — The Listening

Winter. Quiet authority. Your story is somebody else's medicine.

You rest. You listen. You write. Leadership proven by reception, not output.

Quests

  • Sit one full week without posting or recruiting
  • Hold space for others in the circle
  • Write your story in the journal below
Threshold: The Listening. The Lantern rank opens only after the week of rest.
Clan Mother's blessing: "In stillness, you become the medicine."

Journal — "What did you hear in someone else's story that was really yours?"
