Welcome, gentle one

Today is yours to arrive in.

No pressure, no hurry.

A soft moment between you and your inner voice.

You may type a word or two — or go straight in.

First, we zip up

Zip up is how we gather the self.

Feet settled. Spine soft and easy.

A quiet breath, in and out, through the nose. You come fully home into your own body.

Now we calibrate

Calibration finds how your body answers today.

It can shift from day to day. So we ask fresh, every single time. There is no wrong answer here.

Ask your body a quiet yes question — something simple and true, like "is my body present with me right now?" Then notice what moves.

Nothing moved — that is an answer too

The body can be quiet at first. We settle it gently, three soft checks.

Water. A few slow sips, then settle again.

Soft knees. Unlock the knees, soften the jaw, let the shoulders drop.

Posture. Feet flat, spine tall and easy.

Then we retest

A few quiet statements, one at a time.

Ask. Settle. Read the body's answer from today's direction — the one we just found.

Ask your body — does this ring true?
