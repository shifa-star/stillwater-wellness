Meditate

A Slow-Down Series · One

Stillness is where you come back to yourself. A few quiet minutes of sitting with your breath can settle a busy mind, even when it doesn't feel like you are doing anything at all.

You do not need to clear your thoughts or sit for a long time. Find a comfortable place, close your eyes, and let the thoughts come and go like weather. Then, when you are ready, return to your day a little lighter.
