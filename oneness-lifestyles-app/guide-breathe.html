Breathe

A Slow-Down Series · One

Your breath is always with you. A few slow, even breaths can help quiet a busy mind and settle the body before you move on with your day.

Try this: sit comfortably, close your eyes if you like, and take four gentle counts in through your nose, then four slow counts out through your mouth.
