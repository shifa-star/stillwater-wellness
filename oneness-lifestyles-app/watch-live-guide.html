How to Watch Oneness Lifestyles Live

A simple guide for our community

Welcome, Beloved, to your Oneness Wellness online experience. When Clan Mother Shifa and Mz. Felicia go live, here's how you can watch right inside the Oneness Lifestyles app.

📱 Step 1: Open the App

1

Go to the app

Open your browser and go to:
shifa-star.github.io/stillwater-wellness/oneness-lifestyles-app/

🔴 Step 2: Find the Stream Room Button

2

Look at the top-right corner

You'll see a green button that says "Stream Room". Click it!

🎥 Step 3: Watch the Live Stream

3

You're in the Stream Room

When Clan Mother is live, you'll see her video playing right on the page. No need to open Zoom or leave the app!

💡 Did you know? The Stream Room shows your YouTube or Facebook live stream automatically. When she's not live, you can still watch past recordings and follow along on those platforms.

🔗 Step 4: Follow Us Everywhere

4

Stay connected

Click the big buttons at the bottom of the Stream Room to follow on:

  • YouTube — for full recordings and live streams
  • Facebook — for community updates and live sessions

❓ What If It's Not Live?

When Clan Mother isn't broadcasting, the video box will be blank with a message: "Not live right now? Check the recordings below, or follow us on YouTube & Facebook to catch the next one."

That's okay! You can still:

  • Watch past streams on YouTube
  • Follow on Facebook for announcements
  • Join us next time — we'll let you know when we're live!
