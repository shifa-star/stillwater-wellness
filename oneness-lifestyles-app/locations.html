Oneness Wellness Center, Sanctuary of Self Healing

5257 Kalanianaʻole Highway (Simpson Manor), ʻĀina Haina, East Honolulu, HI 96821

A nearly one-acre oceanfront property in the ʻĀina Haina neighborhood of East Honolulu. Its historic former school building is an approximately 29,000 sq. ft. mixed-use space that once housed The Waldorf School and The Academy.

Oneness Wellness Lifestyles @ The Waikiki Lanais

2452 Tusitala St, Honolulu, HI

A mix of rooftop gathering and fitness facilities.
